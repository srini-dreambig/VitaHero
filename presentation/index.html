Specialty-restricted access

When you register a doctor, you pick their specialty. They only see the forms they're qualified to fill.

In the admin portal, each doctor is tagged — Dentist, ENT specialist, Ophthalmologist, Nutritionist, or Paediatrician. The login, the screens, and the form sections are all filtered by that tag. Safe. Clean. Compliant.

Dentist
Dental Check section only
Ophthalmologist
Vision Screening only
ENT
Hearing & General Physical
Nutritionist
Nutrition & Anaemia only
Paediatrician
All sections
No unauthorised access — ever
A dentist cannot enter vision readings. An ENT specialist cannot fill dental findings. A doctor who isn't registered in the admin portal cannot log in at all — even with a valid OTP. The phone number must match a doctor record.

Onboarding you

Three steps, five minutes — you're a VitaHero doctor.

1
Admin registers you
Our team adds your name, phone number, and specialty (Dentist / ENT / Ophthalmologist / Nutritionist / Paediatrician) in the admin portal.
2
You log in with OTP
Open VitaHero, enter your phone number, receive an OTP. VitaHero verifies your number against the doctor record — if it matches, you're in.
3
You see your camps & forms
Your dashboard loads with the camps assigned to your specialty. Your form shows only your sections. You're ready to screen.
If you're not in the admin portal, you can't log in.
Even with a correct OTP, an unregistered phone number is rejected. This is how we keep unauthorised people out of children's health records.
